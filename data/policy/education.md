# Sample Bank: Education Loan Policy

Sample policy, synthetic. Read with the Common Credit Policy. The student is the primary applicant. A co-applicant, usually a parent, is mandatory and is the earner whose income, credit and stability are assessed.

## ED-ELIG-01 | public | Student age
The student must be at least 18 years old at application. The student's age at maturity must not exceed 55.

## ED-ELIG-02 | public | Co-applicant required
A co-applicant is mandatory. The co-applicant must be 21 to 55 at maturity, and gives the same KYC, income proof and bank statement set as a primary applicant. Missing co-applicant documents stop the case until they are uploaded.

## ED-ELIG-03 | public | Co-applicant credit score
The co-applicant's credit score must be 700 or above. Below 700 the recommendation is to reject. A score of 750 or above earns full credit points.

## ED-ELIG-04 | public | Co-applicant income
The co-applicant's income must be proved by payslips or income tax returns, with a bank statement.

## ED-STAB-01 | sample | Co-applicant stability
Stable employment or business of the co-applicant for at least 24 months earns the stability points.

## ED-PROD-01 | public | Moratorium and repayment
The moratorium is the course duration plus one year. Repayment follows and is limited to 15 years. The co-applicant's age at maturity must not exceed 55, which can shorten repayment.

## ED-PROD-02 | RBI | Collateral
No collateral is needed up to Rs 4 lakh, and none is taken for loans up to Rs 7.5 lakh. Collateral is required for loans above Rs 7.5 lakh. Missing required collateral loses policy points.

## ED-PROD-03 | public | Margin
No margin is needed up to Rs 4 lakh. Above Rs 4 lakh the margin is 5 percent for study in India and 15 percent for study abroad. A margin below this loses policy points.

## ED-PROD-04 | sample | Admission and fees
The admission letter, fee structure and marksheets must be present and consistent with the course, institution and total fee on the application form.
