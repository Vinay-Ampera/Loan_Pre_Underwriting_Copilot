# Sample Bank: Business Loan Policy

Sample policy, synthetic. Read with the Common Credit Policy.

## BL-ELIG-01 | public | Age and tenure
Every promoter and co-applicant is 21 to 55 at maturity. Tenure is shortened so that this holds.

## BL-ELIG-02 | public | Eligible applicants
The applicant is self-employed. Promoter KYC, business proof, GST registration or Udyam registration, a 12-month bank statement, 12 months of GST returns, two years of income tax returns, and profit and loss with balance sheet are required.

## BL-ELIG-03 | public | Credit score
A credit score of 650 or above is required. Below 650 the recommendation is to reject. A score of 700 or above earns full credit points.

## BL-ELIG-04 | public | Minimum turnover
Annual turnover must be at least Rs 36 lakh, as shown in GST returns and income tax returns.

## BL-STAB-01 | public | Business vintage
The business must be at least two years old. A longer track record earns the stability points.

## BL-STAB-02 | sample | Banking behaviour
Credits in the bank statement over the last 12 months must be at least 80 percent of the sales reported in GST returns; a lower ratio loses points. Three or more cheque returns in 12 months send the case to refer.

## BL-PROD-01 | sample | Tenure
The maximum tenure is 84 months, subject to the product chosen.

## BL-PROD-02 | public | GST or Udyam
A valid GSTIN or Udyam registration is required. The PAN inside the GSTIN must match the promoter's PAN, and the GSTIN checksum must be valid.

## BL-PROD-03 | sample | Loan against turnover
The loan amount may be up to 30 percent of annual turnover. A higher ratio loses policy points.
