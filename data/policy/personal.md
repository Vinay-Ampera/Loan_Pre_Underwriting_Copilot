# Sample Bank: Personal Loan Policy

Sample policy, synthetic. Read with the Common Credit Policy.

## PL-ELIG-01 | public | Age and tenure
Age at application is 21 to 55, and age at maturity must not exceed 55. The tenure is reduced where needed to satisfy this.

## PL-ELIG-02 | public | Employment
The applicant must be salaried. Payslips for the last three months and a bank statement for six months are required. Form 16 or an income tax return is optional.

## PL-ELIG-03 | public | Credit score
A credit score of 700 or above is acceptable. A score from 650 to 699 is acceptable only as a referral: the case cannot be recommended for approval and goes to refer. A score below 650 is a hard stop and the recommendation is to reject. Higher scores earn more credit points (750 and above is the top tier).

## PL-ELIG-04 | public | Minimum income
Net monthly income must be at least Rs 25,000. Income below this loses the income-floor points.

## PL-STAB-01 | public | Experience and stability
At least two years of total work experience and at least one year with the current employer are expected. Each earns income points.

## PL-PROD-01 | public | Tenure
The maximum tenure is 60 months.

## PL-PROD-02 | sample | Maximum loan amount
The loan amount may be up to Rs 25 lakh. A larger amount loses policy points.

## PL-PROD-03 | sample | Permitted purposes
A personal loan may be used for any lawful personal purpose. It may not be used for speculation, investment in securities, or purchase of a property. A purpose outside the permitted list loses policy points and is flagged.
