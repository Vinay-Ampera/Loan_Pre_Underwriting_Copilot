# Sample Bank: Home Loan Policy

Sample policy, synthetic. Read with the Common Credit Policy.

## HL-ELIG-01 | public | Age and tenure
Age at application is 21 to 55 for every applicant and co-applicant, and age at maturity must not exceed 55. A co-applicant is optional. Where there is one, the same checks apply to that person and the FOIR is combined.

## HL-ELIG-02 | public | Employment
Salaried and self-employed applicants are accepted. Salaried applicants give three months of payslips and Form 16. Self-employed applicants give three years of income tax returns with profit and loss statement and balance sheet. A six-month bank statement is required for both.

## HL-ELIG-03 | public | Credit score
A credit score of 700 or above is required. Below 700 the recommendation is to reject. A score of 750 or above earns full credit points.

## HL-ELIG-04 | public | Minimum income
Net monthly income (combined, where there is a co-applicant) must be at least Rs 25,000.

## HL-STAB-01 | sample | Stability
Stable employment or business for at least 24 months earns the stability points.

## HL-PROD-01 | public | Tenure
The maximum tenure is 30 years, and it is shortened so that age at maturity does not exceed 55 for every applicant.

## HL-PROD-02 | RBI | Loan to value
The maximum loan to value is 90 percent for loans up to Rs 30 lakh, 80 percent for loans above Rs 30 lakh and up to Rs 75 lakh, and 75 percent for loans above Rs 75 lakh. A higher ratio loses policy points.

## HL-PROD-03 | sample | Property documents
The sale agreement or allotment letter, title deed, encumbrance certificate and approved plan must be present and consistent with the application form (property address and owner name). A builder NOC is required where applicable.

## HL-PROD-04 | sample | Existing loans in FOIR
Existing loans count toward FOIR only if more than 12 instalments remain unpaid.
