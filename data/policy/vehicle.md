# Sample Bank: Vehicle Loan Policy

Sample policy, synthetic. Read with the Common Credit Policy. Covers new cars, used cars and two-wheelers.

## VL-ELIG-01 | public | Age and tenure
Age at application is 21 to 55, and age at maturity must not exceed 55.

## VL-ELIG-02 | public | Employment
Salaried and self-employed applicants are accepted. Salaried applicants give three months of payslips. Self-employed applicants give an income tax return. A six-month bank statement and the dealer proforma invoice are required. A driving licence is required for a two-wheeler. The registration certificate and insurance are required for a used vehicle.

## VL-ELIG-03 | public | Credit score
A credit score of 700 or above is required. Below 700 the recommendation is to reject. A score of 750 or above earns full credit points.

## VL-ELIG-04 | public | Minimum income
Salaried net monthly income must be at least Rs 25,000. For self-employed applicants the income is taken from the income tax return and must also be at least Rs 25,000 a month.

## VL-STAB-01 | sample | Stability
Stable employment or business for at least 24 months earns the stability points.

## VL-PROD-01 | public | Tenure
The maximum tenure is 84 months for a new car, 60 months for a used car and 60 months for a two-wheeler.

## VL-PROD-02 | public | Loan to value
The maximum loan is 85 percent of the on-road price for a new vehicle and 80 percent for a used vehicle. A higher ratio loses policy points.

## VL-PROD-03 | sample | Vehicle documents
The dealer proforma invoice must match the make, model and on-road price on the application form. A driving licence (two-wheeler) and the registration certificate and insurance (used vehicle) must be valid.
